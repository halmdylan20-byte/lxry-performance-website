Caffeine + L-Theanine

Research suggests that combining caffeine and L-theanine may support alertness and attention during mentally demanding tasks.

A randomized controlled study published in Nutritional Neuroscience reported improvements in certain measures of task-switching accuracy and subjective alertness among participants who consumed caffeine and L-theanine together.

Scientific reference: Giesbrecht et al. (2010), Nutritional Neuroscience.

Results vary by individual and task. Research on these ingredients does not establish that the finished LXRY formula improves athletic performance.
